Questions barbers ask

Can it connect to my booking app?

Yes. We link your existing booking app (Square, Booksy, Vagaro, Fresha, StyleSeat or another) to a clear Book button, or embed it where the app allows.

I already have Instagram. Why do I need a website?

Instagram is great for showing your work, but Google ranks websites for searches like “barber near me”. A site with your hours, prices and address helps you show up there, and it can show your Instagram work too.

Can each barber have a section?

Yes. Each barber can get a short profile, their specialties and their own booking link.

How can my website be live the same day?

Send your logo, photos, services and hours by 12 pm Eastern on a weekday and your site goes live that evening. Connecting a domain you already own can take a few extra hours.

Do I pay before I see anything?

No. We design a free concept of your homepage first. If you want it, you pay a 50% deposit. If you don't like the first design, the full deposit comes back.
