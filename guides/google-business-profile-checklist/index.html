Google Business Profile Checklist for Local Businesses

A plain-English checklist to get more calls from Google Maps: categories, hours, photos, reviews, and how your website supports your profile.

By George, founder of Storefront Studio. Updated October 2026.

Why it matters

When someone searches for a business like yours nearby, Google often shows a map with three businesses before any website. Your Google Business Profile decides whether you're one of them.

1. Claim and verify

Search your business name on Google Maps. If you see “Own this business?”, claim it and follow Google's verification steps.

2. Pick the right primary category

Choose the one category that best describes what you do, for example “Barber shop” or “Roofing contractor”. Add a few accurate secondary categories.

3. Make hours match everywhere

Your hours on Google, your website, Yelp and Facebook should be identical. Add holiday hours before each holiday.

4. Add real photos

Your storefront, your team and your work. Add new ones regularly.

5. Ask happy customers for reviews

Ask in person or with a short link after a good visit. Reply to every review, good or bad, politely. Never buy or fake reviews.

6. Link a fast website

Your profile's website link should go to a fast, mobile-friendly page that repeats your name, address, phone, hours and services. That consistency helps Google trust your listing.
